# Theme philosophy pressure profiles

Profiles are opposing constraint sets for escaping default taste. They are not
recipes and deliberately specify no fonts, colors, radii, or component choices.
Select one, combine two with an explicit tension, or choose `none` and author a
new profile. Never silently choose `be-Mark` or any other personal house style.

## Luminous console

- Intent: make operation, status, and information feel instrumented.
- Pressure: dark luminance structure, scarce high-chroma signals, mechanical
  geometry, compact controls, directional indicators, decisive state changes.
- Refuses: neon wallpaper, generic rounded SaaS controls, decorative sci-fi
  noise, weak reading text.

## Industrial instrument

- Intent: feel durable, repairable, and legible under pressure.
- Pressure: obvious affordances, hard material seams, substantial targets,
  numbered hierarchy, redundant state signals, restrained motion.
- Refuses: precious surfaces, ambiguous icon-only controls, hairline contrast,
  polished lifestyle branding.

## Reader-first institutional

- Intent: establish trust through patient reading and durable structure.
- Pressure: strong measure and rhythm, sober hierarchy, generous targets,
  explicit labels, calm state deltas, print-aware prose.
- Refuses: tiny metadata, novelty motion, ornamental density, authority conveyed
  only through dark blue.

## Playful utility

- Intent: make routine actions clear, buoyant, and forgiving.
- Pressure: friendly silhouettes, expressive yet redundant indicators, visible
  feedback, varied scale, comfortable spacing, energetic composition.
- Refuses: childish copy, color-only state, low-density toy UI, identical pills
  for every control.

## Editorial luxury

- Intent: create drama through hierarchy, pacing, and material restraint.
- Pressure: typographic contrast, asymmetry, deliberate emptiness, sharp image
  framing, selective ornament, composed prose.
- Refuses: illegibly small labels, generic beige minimalism, ornamental serif on
  every surface, low-contrast fashion conventions.

## Severe modernist

- Intent: expose structure and remove unearned decoration.
- Pressure: grid logic, blunt hierarchy, strict alignment, limited material
  vocabulary, immediate interaction feedback, rigorous spacing.
- Refuses: neutral template sameness, vague hierarchy, accidental softness,
  austerity used to excuse inaccessible targets.

## Expressive postmodern

- Intent: let components disagree productively while preserving a coherent rule
  system.
- Pressure: unexpected alignment, mixed scales, varied silhouettes, visible
  layers, distinct indicator families, choreographed disruption.
- Refuses: random decoration, novelty that obscures semantics, one eccentric font
  carrying the entire theme, motion without a reduced alternative.

## None

- Intent: author a distinct philosophy from the product or cultural context.
- Pressure: state the thesis, tensions, and refusals before choosing values.
- Refuses: treating personal taste, current design trends, or the font pairing as
  sufficient creative direction.
